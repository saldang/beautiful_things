`Type:` grilling
`Status:` open
`Blocked by:` —
`Blocks:` 05

## Question

What exactly is an "update", who is allowed to publish one as a charity, and how social does the network actually get?

To decide:

- Authoring: one shared org account, or org accounts with member roles (a volunteer vs an officer)? Does a poster's name/role appear?
- Shape of an update: short text only, text + images, links, attachments? Editing and deleting after publish — and whether edits are visible as history (the destination history set a precedent).
- Feed: strictly chronological by follow, or ranked? (If ranked, ticket 05 must also govern the feed, not just discovery.)
- Ordinary users' surface area: follow only, or also reactions, comments, reposts, direct messages? Every one of these is a moderation obligation; the minimum that makes the network feel alive is the right answer here.
- Notifications: what leaves the app (email/push), and what that costs to run — the cost tracker has to be able to show it.
- Drafts, scheduling, and whether an update can be pinned/featured by the org.
